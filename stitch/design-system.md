## North Star

The design system frames the product as an "Architectural Intellectual" workspace: calm, editorial, premium, and structured through tonal layering rather than hard borders.

## Design Rules

- Avoid heavy 1px borders as section dividers.
- Use tonal contrast between surfaces to separate modules.
- Prefer ambient, diffused shadow over dark drop shadow.
- Use generous whitespace to create an editorial feel.
- Inputs should feel quiet and integrated into the canvas.
- Active states should lean on subtle background shifts and primary color accents.

## Signature Detail

- Use a soft `primary_fixed` glow (`#c4e7ff`) behind active AI blocks or focused interaction zones when emphasis is needed.
